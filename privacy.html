The Boli Bureaucrat
Privacy policy

The Boli Bureaucrat is an offline political career simulation from The Boli Bureaucrat team. Contact us at shahydlegacyofficial@gmail.com.

Information on your device

The game stores your chosen character name, education and skills, optional portrait, settings, and simulated career progress locally. You can use a fictional name and a bundled portrait. The app does not require an account.

Camera and photos

Taking or choosing a portrait is optional. The app opens the device camera or system photo picker only when you choose that action. A selected image is cropped and resized on the device for your character portrait. The game does not upload the photo or access your wider photo library. It does not save camera images to your public gallery.

No publisher collection or tracking

This release has no advertising, analytics, cloud AI, cloud saves, remote game server, or in-app purchases. The game team does not receive your character details, photos, gameplay, device identifiers, or usage telemetry from the game. The local encounter director works on your device.

Save exports and sharing

When you explicitly export a career, its JSON backup includes the character name, optional portrait, preferences, and game progress. On Android the system share sheet lets you choose where to send or save it. A receiving service has its own privacy policy. Keep exported files private if they include your real name or image. Import reads only a backup you select.

Fictional social feed

Island Buzz posts and replies are local game events. They are not published to any external social network. The game does not contain a real-world political source browser.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you replace or delete it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled for this release. Exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them; delete them separately. Because we do not operate game accounts or collect game data, there is no server account to delete.

Audience and communications

The game is intended for players aged 16 and over and contains fictional political misconduct and detention themes. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond. Do not send sensitive personal information or a career export unless needed for your request.

Changes and contact

We will update the effective date if this policy changes. Privacy questions can be sent to shahydlegacyofficial@gmail.com.
